Digital Object 1: Spatial Canvases

Two short studies in P5.js exploring spatial composition through primitive geometry, layered color, motion, and cursor interaction.

Static Study: Folded Field

A static spatial drawing built only from primitive shapes: circles, rectangles, triangles, arcs, and lines arranged as a layered architectural field.

Interactive Study: Signal Garden

An animated drawing where moving particles orbit through a gridded landscape and respond to the mouse position with shifting color and scale.
